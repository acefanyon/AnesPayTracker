## Description

Character count: ~2,400 / 4,000 maximum.

```

Track your anesthesia shifts and know exactly what you should be paid — across every employer, site, and bonus structure.

AnesPayTracker helps CRNAs and anesthesiologists log shifts, calculate pay, track bonuses and streaks, and estimate future paychecks before they arrive. If you work at multiple hospitals or surgery centers with different pay rates, bonus rules, and payout schedules, this app keeps it all organized.

WHAT IT DOES

• Log shifts in seconds — per day, per hour, or with clock-in/clock-out times and ceiling quarter-hour rounding
• Track on-call shifts, splash bonuses, and custom named bonuses per employer
• Set up per-employer streak rules — "work X days in a rolling window and earn $Y per day after"
• Every bonus type supports its own aggregation and payout schedule — paid with the shift, aggregated monthly and paid the last check of next month, or aggregated quarterly and paid the last check of the month following the quarter
• Per-day bonuses can optionally prorate for half-day or partial-day shifts so your numbers match your paycheck
• Hourly shifts include optional clock-in/clock-out entry with quarter-hour ceiling rounding (8.01 becomes 8.25)

REPORTS

• Earnings report — see base pay, bonuses, and streaks broken down by type for any month, quarter, pay period, or custom range
• Bonus Payout report — see which bonuses are scheduled to pay out during a given period, grouped by bonus type and payout schedule
• Paycheck Estimator — the critical one. For each employer, configure a paycheck anchor date and delay rule (e.g. work in pay period A is paid when period B closes). The estimator shows every line item — base pay, on-call, custom bonuses, and streak bonuses — grouped under each expected paycheck date, with the aggregation window behind it

EXPORT

• Export any report to PDF — earnings, bonus payouts, or paycheck estimator — with full breakdown tables
• Share individual shifts as .ics calendar files
• Opt-in native calendar sync via Settings

BUILT FOR REAL CONTRACTS

• Every employer can have its own pay cadence, bonus types, streak rules, and paycheck timing
• Per-day, per-hour, flat, and prorated bonuses all handled
• On-call bonuses never prorate
• Streak bonuses prorate by day fraction
• History safety — editing an employer or its rules does not change past shifts; shift pay snapshots are preserved at the time of entry
• Paycheck Estimator warns you when an employer has not yet configured a paycheck anchor date

AnesPayTracker stores your data locally on your device. No account required. No ads. No subscriptions. Your shift and pay data stays with you.

Built by a practicing anesthesia provider who understands the real-world complexity of multi-site, multi-employer pay structures.
```

## Privacy Policy (suggested)

The app stores shift and pay data locally. Calendar access permission is requested only when the user opts in to calendar sync. No data is transmitted to the developer. A simple privacy policy page stating this (hosted on GitHub Pages or similar) satisfies Apple's requirement.
